How it works

A coach, not a note-taker

Notes apps remember the meeting. Wes changes how you sound in the next one.

●

Record without a bot

Your mic is you; system audio is them. Nothing joins the meeting, nothing appears in the participant list.

✎

Transcribe on your Mac

Whisper runs locally. Audio is deleted the moment it becomes text — it never rests on disk, never leaves the machine.

✓

Get coached, measurably

Hard metrics — pace, fillers, talk ratio, interruptions — plus the two or three moments that mattered, judged against real executive-communication frameworks.

The flywheel

It learns your patterns, not your voice

Every call and email feeds a private coaching profile of your recurring weaknesses — the hedge you always open with, the ask you always bury. That’s how Wes can say “you did it again” — and prove it when you improve. It never mimics you; its job is to move you off those habits.

Privacy

Local-first, or it doesn’t ship

  • Audio never leaves your Mac — transcribed locally, deleted after transcription.
  • You start every recording. No always-on capture, no silent auto-record.
  • Only transcripts are analyzed — sent to the model you configure, with your key.
  • One pane lists everything stored. Delete any call, or everything, any time.
